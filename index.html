Good to know

Questions we get asked.

Do you charge for a quote?

No. Quotes are free and written, and there's no call-out fee for coming to look at a job.

How long does a fuse board upgrade take?

Most domestic consumer unit changes are done in a single day. Power is off for a few hours while we swap the board and test every circuit.

Do landlords need an EICR?

Yes. Private landlords in England must have a valid EICR for every tenancy, renewed at least every five years, and provide a copy to tenants and to the council on request.

Are you registered and insured?

We're registered with NICEIC and NAPIT, which means our work is assessed and the certificates we issue are recognised by councils, mortgage lenders and insurers. We carry full public liability cover.

Can you work with my builder or main contractor?

Yes. A large part of our work is on commercial new-builds, extensions and refits alongside main contractors, following site RAMS and the contractor's programme.
